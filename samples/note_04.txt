was at a trade fair in Bengaluru last week, small skincare/wellness expo. one of the booths had a huge banner - "DERMATOLOGICALLY TESTED" - on what looked like a body lotion. asked the person at the booth what that meant, tested by whom, how many people, what protocol. she genuinely didn't know, said it was on the packaging from the manufacturer. not her fault at all, she's sales staff not a formulator, but nobody up the chain had apparently thought this needed answering either. "dermatologically tested" isn't a regulated claim in India the way people assume it is - it could mean one paid dermatologist looked at it once, or a proper 50-person patch test, or nothing at all. good industry transparency post, similar shape to the clinically tested one we already did but a different claim so it doesn't feel repetitive.
