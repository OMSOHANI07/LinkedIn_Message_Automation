customer support flagged something today - a customer asked why her "10% vitamin c serum" from some other brand stings so much she stopped using it after 3 days. reminded me that vitamin c products vary massively - the L-AA form degrades so fast and is so pH dependent, versus the derivative forms which are gentler but weaker. the label just says "10%" and that number alone tells you almost nothing about what she's actually going to experience on her face. worth writing about - the % is not the story, stability and form and delivery are.
